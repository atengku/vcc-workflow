Managing the Unknowns:
a VCC engine, deployed under your brand

A white-labelled client-intake and draft-assist engine for Singapore VCC formation, built to win the volume tier and free your lawyers for the work that pays.

01The offer

Managing the Unknowns is a client-facing engine you deploy under your own brand. A prospective manager answers a short set of questions and the engine returns a structured scoping brief, a workstream map, a generated first-draft document set, and the list of open decisions your lawyers resolve. The constitution is adapted from the Singapore Academy of Law model; the information memorandum, subscription agreement, fund management agreement and side letter are produced from maintained bases.

The division of labour is the point. The engine drafts the standard eighty percent and surfaces the rest; your lawyers own the bespoke twenty percent and the review. It is positioned throughout as scoping and draft-assist under your supervision, never as legal advice, which keeps it on the correct side of the rules on unauthorised practice and makes you, not the tool, the source of the advice.

02Why it makes sense for you

  • 1A client-acquisition funnel. The branded intake converts a vague enquiry into a qualified, structured instruction, capturing the manager earlier and shortening the sales cycle.
  • 2Margin and junior leverage. Associates review generated drafts instead of building from a blank page, so you can offer competitive fixed-fee VCC setups without burning partner time.
  • 3Defence against the volume tier. Fund administrators and corporate service providers are absorbing standardised setup. A productised offering lets you compete for that tail rather than cede it.
  • 4Client experience. A polished, firm-branded scoping flow where larger competitors still send a Word questionnaire by email.
  • 5A scalable service line. One engine powers many setups, turning bespoke project work into a repeatable VCC product with predictable economics.

03The model

Structure  White-label deployment under your brand. Seeking Alpha Advisors builds, hosts, maintains and updates the engine as ACRA and MAS rules change. You supply precedent quality and the counsel-review backstop, and you own the client relationship and the advice. Commercials by revenue share or a per-fund or per-seat subscription, to be agreed.

04The ask

A thirty-minute walkthrough of the live engine, followed by a scoped pilot on a small number of VCC setups to measure time saved and conversion lift. No platform commitment until the pilot proves out.

06Sequencing the outreach

Lead with the two boutiques, PDLegal and CNPLaw: clearest VCC-plus-family-office positioning and the strongest incentive to adopt a client-acquisition funnel. Hold Bird & Bird ATMD as the technology-credibility play, pitched as a programme fit. Treat Withers KhattarWong as the client-overlap prize but pitch only the funnel.
