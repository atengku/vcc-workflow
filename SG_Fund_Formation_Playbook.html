Singapore Fund Formation:
Workflow, Documents & Variable Map

A staged decision map for constituting a Singapore investment fund. Each node sets out the documents produced, the variable inputs that drive branching, the legal scenarios that result, and the source references. The default spine is the Variable Capital Company (VCC), with the limited partnership, unit trust and private company treated as alternative vehicles at Node 0. Document skeletons brief counsel: they are structural outlines, not execution-ready legal drafts.

Workflow

The formation spine as nodes, with documents, variables and scenarios
00
Threshold · Vehicle Selection
Choose the legal wrapper

If the arrangement pools capital, is managed as a whole, and the participants lack day-to-day control, it is a collective investment scheme (CIS) and falls under the Securities and Futures Act.4 The VCC was introduced as a body corporate purpose-built for CIS, sitting alongside the limited partnership, limited liability partnership and company as the onshore options.5 More than 1,400 VCCs were established by Q1 2025, reflecting the shift away from the older LP and Cayman segregated-portfolio structures.17

01
Precondition · Fund Manager
Secure the regulated manager

A VCC cannot self-manage: it must appoint a Permissible Fund Manager regulated by MAS, which shapes the entire timeline.11 The lighter-touch Registered Fund Management Company (RFMC) regime was repealed on 1 August 2024; existing RFMCs had to transition to an Accredited/Institutional Licensed Fund Management Company via Form 1AR in the 1 April to 30 June 2024 window.6 New entrants now apply directly for a Capital Markets Services (CMS) licence.8

Documents produced
  • CMS licence application Form 1A (new) / Form 1AR (RFMC transition)
  • Business plan, financial projections, AUM build applicant-prepared
  • Compliance manual A/I LFMC standard
  • AML / CFT policies and procedures SFA + MAS Notices
  • Risk management framework
  • IT / cybersecurity policy MAS technology risk
  • Base capital injection evidence and fit-and-proper declarations directors, reps, shareholders
Variable inputs at this node
V4 Investor classA/I LFMC serves accredited and institutional only; Retail LFMC reaches all investors at materially higher capital and conduct cost
V8 StrategyVC-only portfolios can use the simplified Venture Capital Fund Manager (VCFM) regime, exempt from some capital and conduct rules
V5 RouteOwn licence (6 to 9 months for A/I; 9 to 15 months retail) vs hosting under an existing licensed manager
SCENARIO A · OWN LICENCE Application fee S$1,000 per regulated activity; annual S$4,000 to S$8,000 (A/I) or S$8,000+ (retail); indicative advisory S$80,000 to S$180,000.8 Transitioned A/I LFMCs carry a S$250m AUM cap as a licence condition.6
SCENARIO B · HOSTED MANAGER Launch the VCC under an existing MAS-licensed manager, removing the cost and lead time of obtaining a CMS licence. Common for emerging and external managers.11
SCENARIO C · FAMILY OFFICE A single-family office is generally not a Permissible Fund Manager, so it must engage an external licensed manager or obtain its own licence.9
02
Constitution · Incorporation
Incorporate with ACRA

The constitution must be prepared in advance and filed with the incorporation application through ACRA's VCC portal, a register separate from ordinary companies.3 The Singapore Academy of Law publishes two model constitutions, one for open-ended and one for closed-end funds, which can be adopted or replaced with a bespoke draft.10 Name reservation is processed in about one working day and holds for 120 days.3 The VCC incorporation fee is indicatively S$8,000 with processing around 14 business days, subject to completeness.12

Documents produced
  • Name reservation application ACRA VCC portal
  • VCC Constitution SAL model (open / closed) or bespoke
  • Incorporation application ACRA · fee ~S$8,000
  • Particulars of directors, secretary and Permissible Fund Manager
  • Consents to act and fit-and-proper declarations
  • Registered office (physical SG address; no P.O. box)9
  • Sub-fund registration form umbrella only · S$400 per sub-fund
  • Transfer-of-registration application + certified foreign constitution & incorporation certificate re-domiciliation only
Variable inputs at this node
V2 ShapeStandalone vs umbrella; each sub-fund registered separately and segregated by statute (s 29 VCC Act)
V3 LiquidityOpen vs closed-end selects which SAL model constitution applies
V4 Investor classAn authorised (retail) scheme requires at least three directors including one independent; otherwise one director suffices
V6 OriginFresh incorporation vs transfer of registration of a comparable foreign corporate fund
SCENARIO · UMBRELLA One legal entity, multiple sub-funds, each with segregated assets and liabilities; a sub-fund has no separate legal personality but is treated as separate for insolvency and enforcement.13 Tax incentives are assessed at the umbrella level.
SCENARIO · RE-DOMICILIATION Only a foreign fund with a corporate structure equivalent to a VCC may transfer its registration to Singapore; notify the foreign authority of de-registration.10
03
Governance · Mandatory Appointments
Stand up the officers and providers

The VCC must bolt on statutory infrastructure that an ordinary company is not forced to carry: a MAS-regulated manager, qualifying directors, a company secretary, an auditor, and the administrator and custodian that run the fund day to day.11 At least one director must be ordinarily resident in Singapore, and at least one director must be a director or qualified representative of the fund manager.9 Appointment deadlines bite: company secretary within six months, auditor within three months, and unlike a small private company a VCC has no audit exemption.9

Documents produced
  • Board resolutions appointing directors, secretary, auditor
  • Fund Management Agreement VCC ↔ manager
  • Fund Administration Agreement NAV, registry, investor services
  • Custodian Agreement approved custodian / approved trustee
  • Auditor engagement letter SG-approved public accounting firm
  • Banking and account-opening pack fresh KYC per sub-fund
Variable inputs at this node
V4 Investor classAuthorised scheme requires the trustee/custodian and CIS-Code-compliant governance; three directors including one independent
V8 StrategyHedge strategies add a prime brokerage agreement and rehypothecation limits
Slow leg
Bank and custody account opening is frequently the longest part of the timeline, each sub-fund being a fresh KYC and risk assessment, so it should start in parallel from day one rather than at the end.11
04
Offer · Investor Documents
Build the offer pack

The offer documents fork sharply on investor class. A CIS offered in Singapore must be authorised (if SG-constituted) or recognised (if foreign), and the offer must be made with a MAS-registered prospectus and product highlights sheet, unless an exemption applies.4 Authorisation runs through Form 1 on the OPERA system, conditioned on a CMS-licensed manager and an appointed approved custodian for a VCC or sub-fund.4 A restricted offer to accredited or institutional investors, or at a minimum of S$200,000 per investor, can instead rely on an information memorandum under the private-placement exemptions.39

Documents produced
  • Prospectus + Product Highlights Sheet retail / authorised · OPERA Form 1
  • Private Placement Memorandum / Information Memorandum restricted / A-I offer
  • Subscription Agreement per investor
  • Investor questionnaire / Accredited Investor declaration KYC + suitability
  • Side letters anchor LPs only · MFN, fee, key-person
  • Summary of terms / term sheet, marketing deck counsel-reviewed
Variable inputs at this node
V4 Investor classRetail triggers prospectus + PHS + Code on CIS; A/I triggers IM + exemption reliance
V9 GeographyCross-border investors pull in foreign offer rules and broaden the AML/KYC perimeter
Contrarian flag
The PPM/IM is the impressive document but not the legally indispensable one. In the general private-fund stack the constitution (here, the VCC constitution) is what binds the deal; where no PPM is used, the disclosures and risk factors must sit inside the subscription agreement instead.18 Do not let a polished IM mask a thin constitution.
05
Integrity · AML/CFT & Ownership
Wire the compliance perimeter

Under MAS Notice VCC-N01 the VCC is subject to AML/CFT obligations and in practice must outsource them to an eligible financial institution, usually the fund manager or a qualified corporate service provider, which performs customer due diligence, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting.9 The VCC remains ultimately responsible even where it delegates.10 It must maintain a register of beneficial owners, which is disclosed to authorities on request though not made public.2

Documents produced
  • AML/CFT outsourcing agreement to eligible FI · MAS Notice VCC-N01
  • Register of members private
  • Register of beneficial owners / controllers
  • Register of charges if borrowing · file with ACRA within 30 days
06
Optional Parallel · Tax Incentive
Apply for 13O / 13U

Two headline incentives, administered jointly by IRAS and MAS, run in parallel with incorporation rather than after it.9 Section 13O (onshore, formerly 13R) requires at least S$20m AUM at application and S$200,000 of local business spend per year. Section 13U (enhanced tier, formerly 13X) requires at least S$50m AUM and S$500,000 of local spend, with no investor-residency restriction.9 The manager can separately seek the Financial Sector Incentive (FSI-FM) concessionary rate.17

Variable inputs at this node
V7 Tax postureAUM scale and local-spend capacity decide 13O vs 13U vs none
V9 Geography13U removes the investor-residency restriction that 13O effectively imposes
2025 refresh · verify before relying
The 2025 framework moved local-spend to a tiered basis keyed to AUM, allowed an election so incentives do not lapse merely because AUM falls below threshold during the divestment phase, and relaxed the strict MAS-approved-investment-strategy requirement provided activity stays within the offering document's scope.19 Confirm current thresholds against the live MAS circular before filing.
07
Launch & Ongoing
First close to perpetual compliance

Launch is the start of an ongoing cycle, not the finish line: first subscriptions, first NAV, then perpetual filing.11 Each sub-fund of an umbrella VCC must be registered within seven days of establishment, and the umbrella must disclose the segregation of sub-fund assets to contractual counterparties.10 Financial statements must be audited for each sub-fund, the annual return filed with ACRA within seven months of financial year-end, and an AGM is not mandatory if statements are sent to members within five months of year-end and no member requests one.12

Recurring documents
  • Audited financial statements per sub-fund
  • ACRA annual return within 7 months of FYE
  • MAS periodic returns quarterly / annual for LFMC
  • Capital account statements and investor reporting
  • Sub-fund registration filings within 7 days of establishment

Skeletons

Document skeletons for the bespoke instruments

These are clause/section maps to brief counsel and to structure a data room, not execution-ready drafts. A VCC constitution in particular should start from the SAL model rather than a blank page.

// VCC CONSTITUTION — section map
1. Name, registered office, status as VCC under VCC Act 2018
2. Sole purpose: one or more collective investment schemes
3. Standalone vs umbrella; sub-fund schedule, segregation of assets & liabilities (s 29)
4. Share capital: no par value; shares issued/redeemed at NAV; classes
5. Issue, redemption, repurchase mechanics; pricing and dealing days
6. Dividends out of capital and/or profit
7. Board: number, residency, manager-linked director; (retail: 3 incl 1 independent)
8. Delegation to Permissible Fund Manager; custodian appointment
9. Members' rights, meetings, registers (member register private)
10. NAV, valuation policy, accounting standard (SG / IFRS / US GAAP)
11. Amendment (ordinary resolution; limited director-only amendment cases)
12. Winding up / sub-fund termination (Companies Act regime, modified)
// INFORMATION MEMORANDUM (PPM) — section map
1. Important notices, selling restrictions, exemption relied upon (s 304/305)
2. Summary of terms (mirror the constitution; do not diverge)
3. Investment objective, strategy, asset class, restrictions
4. Manager & key persons; track record; conflicts of interest
5. Fees: management fee, carried interest / performance fee, expenses
6. Subscription, redemption, lock-ups, gates, side-pocket policy
7. Risk factors (market, liquidity, key-person, operational, tax, regulatory)
8. Valuation & NAV; service providers (admin, custodian, auditor)
9. Tax (SG incentive posture; investor-level treatment)
10. AML/CFT; data; reporting cadence
// SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT — section map
1. Subscription amount, share class, dealing date
2. Investor identity & legal status; accredited / institutional eligibility
3. Representations & warranties (sophistication, source of funds, sanctions)
4. AML/KYC questionnaire and supporting documents
5. Acknowledgement of IM risk factors (or embedded disclosures if no IM)
6. Power of attorney to the manager for prescribed acts
7. Transfer restrictions; data consent; governing law (Singapore)
// FUND MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT — section map
1. Appointment & scope of discretionary authority; investment mandate
2. Compliance with VCC Act, SFA, CMS-licence conditions
3. Fees & expense allocation; high-water mark / hurdle
4. Delegation, sub-advisory, and oversight of delegates
5. Standard of care, indemnity, liability cap, key-person clause
6. Term, termination, and orderly hand-over of records
// SIDE LETTER — section map
1. Identified anchor investor; incorporation into constitution/IM by reference
2. Economic terms: fee discount, carry adjustment, capacity right
3. Most-Favoured-Nation clause (scope and election mechanics)
4. Key-person redemption / co-investment rights
5. Reporting, transparency, regulatory-status carve-outs
Keep to true anchor LPs; side letters compound governance complexity.

Sequence

Why the order matters

The single most common sequencing error is treating the manager and the bank/custody accounts as late-stage admin. They are the two slow legs. The realistic end-to-end timeline is governed by standing up or hosting the manager, opening bank and custody accounts (a fresh KYC per sub-fund), and, if wanted, securing a tax incentive: the ACRA incorporation step itself is fast once documents are ready.11 Start Node 1 and the banking thread on day one, in parallel, not at the end.

A second discipline carried from general fund practice: align the IM, the constitution and actual operating practice. Inconsistency between the offer document and the governing document is a primary source of investor disputes, so the IM should be drafted to mirror the constitution, not to lead it.18
