34 The framework went from an 18-manager pilot in 2020 to more than 1,400 VCCs and roughly 2,000 sub-funds by Q1 2025, managed by around 600 fund managers and serviced by a documented 250-plus providers. The deepest single fact for strategy: more than 70 percent of alternative funds sold in Singapore are still corporate-form funds domiciled offshore, which is the migration pool the onshore VCC is built to capture.

01Entry nodes: how you become a VCC, and the legal work each triggers

Node A · New launch
Emerging or established manager constitutes a fresh VCC.Legal: vehicle selection, constitution, incorporation, CMS licensing or hosting agreement, offer documents, subscription docs, 13O/13U application, AML setup.
Node B · Re-domiciliation
Offshore corporate fund (Cayman SPC, BVI) transfers registration onshore.Legal: transfer-of-registration pack (ACRA fee ~S$9,000), constitution replacing foreign articles, foreign de-registration, solvency and audit-exemption assessment. The largest pool by far.
Node C · SG restructuring
Existing SG limited partnership, unit trust or company moves to a VCC.Legal: there is no direct conversion under the current Act, so a new VCC plus asset-transfer and restructuring agreements, with tax advice. MAS is reported to be considering allowing conversion, which would open a one-time wave.5
Node D · Family office
Single or multi-family office formalises into a VCC sub-fund.Legal: sub-fund constitution terms, 13O/13U application, governance and in-specie contribution documents, AML.
Node E · Host then graduate
Emerging manager launches a sub-fund under a platform umbrella, then graduates to its own VCC and licence.Legal: hosting agreement first; later own incorporation, licence application and migration of the sub-fund.
Node F · Scale up
Standalone VCC adds sub-funds and becomes an umbrella.Legal: sub-fund registration (ACRA ~S$400 each), new offer documents per sub-fund, segregation disclosures. This is the bridge into the expansion work overleaf.

3 The strategic read: a single umbrella VCC is not one mandate but a stream. The umbrella structure exists precisely so sub-funds share a board, manager and providers, which means the same client returns to the same providers repeatedly. Capturing the formation is the foot in the door; capturing the expansion is the business.

05Where the Alphas are

Alpha 1The Migration Node
Highest value
More than 70 percent of alternative funds sold in Singapore are still offshore corporate funds, and rising Cayman audit and substance costs are closing the gap to onshore. Owning the offshore-to-VCC re-domiciliation flow is the single largest, highest-value, most underserved entry point.3
Alpha 2The Sub-Fund Factory
Annuity
Each umbrella spawns sub-funds repeatedly. The recurring, standardised expansion work is where lifetime value sits, not the one-off formation. Win the umbrella, harvest the sub-funds.
Alpha 3The Orchestration Layer
Platform
The 250-plus providers are fragmented and nobody owns the workflow connecting manager, administrator, counsel and MAS. The intake-and-routing layer (the engine and Managing the Unknowns) can be the connective tissue that directs work across the cottage industry and clips both setup and recurring spend.
Alpha 4The Volume Tail
Underserved
Around 600 managers and 2,000-plus family offices are price-sensitive and underserved by the top firms. A productised fixed-fee offering wins the standardised tail the Big Four will not chase.
Alpha 5The Conversion Catalyst
Optionality
If MAS permits conversion of existing SG companies and unit trusts into VCCs, a one-time migration wave opens. Positioning the orchestration layer ahead of that rule change is a cheap call option.5

07Read and recommendation

Bull
The orchestration layer becomes the default intake for migration and sub-fund work across several providers, clipping a recurring fee on a large share of formations and expansions.
Base
The engine wins the volume tail through one or two partner firms and a platform, a profitable niche capturing setup intake plus sub-fund recurring on a modest share.
Bear
The bundlers (hosted-manager platforms and administrators) build or buy the same intake layer and fold it into their own stack, leaving no independent orchestration gap.
Play Alpha 1 and Alpha 3 together: aim the engine at the offshore-to-VCC migration node and position it as the orchestration layer across the cottage industry, monetised on recurring sub-fund expansion (Alpha 2), starting with the underserved volume tail (Alpha 4). The bundlers are both the competition and the most likely acquirer, which is itself a strategic exit path.
